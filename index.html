chapter one · the person

A little introduction.

Not a card in the archive — just me, saying hello.

hello, lovely human

I'm Mingsi Chen ♡

Allow me to introduce myself. My name is Mingsi Ellevelyn Yùvette Chen, though you can simply call me Mingsi, Mimi, Elle, Eve, Yuve, Ette, or Eyin but -- if you're feeling like giving me a little bit of oomph, Ms. Chen will do just fine. ♡

I'm fluent in both Indonesian and English, so feel free to speak in whichever language comes naturally to you. I enjoy meaningful conversations, playful banter, and getting to know the person behind the first impression. My love languages are Quality Time and Acts of Service, so if you're someone who enjoys long talks and little moments that somehow become unforgettable, I think we'll get along just fine.

When I'm not working, you'll probably find me singing along to my favorite songs, writing whenever inspiration decides to visit, or getting lost in a good musical. I have a soft spot for mystery and romance films, and my playlists are often filled with Isabel LaRosa and songs best enjoyed late at night. I love sharing music, exchanging recommendations, and listening to people passionately talk about the things they love. Honestly, there's something surprisingly attractive about someone who speaks with genuine excitement.

My current listens include Allegra Jordyn, Isabel LaRosa, Faouzia, Adela, Madison Beer, and Olivia Rodrigo, among others. I also adore SYML, AURORA, Agnes Obel, Sufjan Stevens, Dove Cameron, Bon Iver, and classical music. My taste is all over the place, so perhaps we can find something in common? There are so many favorites I can't possibly fit them all here without turning this introduction into a ten-page novel. So, shall we explore our music tastes together?

People describe me as warm, adaptable, and just a little bit of a tease once I feel comfortable. I appreciate people who communicate well, know how to laugh at the little things, and aren't afraid to be themselves. So, if you enjoy good conversations, spontaneous playlists, or simply having someone to brighten your day, I'd be more than happy to keep you company.

Who knows? Maybe you'll become my favorite notification before either of us realizes it. And who knows, I say, but the world already knows that I'll be enthralled and completely smitten with you in a matter of seconds. I'm also a little protective and possessive, but I have excellent self-control, my dear. If you tell me no, then no it is. I trust my dearest to keep their eyes only on me, yes? ♡

Until then, I hope to see you soon, my favorite notification.
